About Send

Send by GroupChatOn is a free tool for moving files and notes directly between two devices, from a phone to a laptop in the same room or from an office PC in India to a family computer in America.

We built it because the usual ways to move a file are clumsy or nosy: emailing yourself hits size limits, chat apps compress photos, and cloud drives keep copies of everything. Send skips all of that: two devices, one code, a direct encrypted line.

Our promises

  • Free to use, with no account.
  • Your files are never uploaded or stored.
  • No ads or trackers on the transfer screen.
  • Plain explanations of how it works and what its limits are.
